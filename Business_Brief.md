# E-commerce Sales Analysis — Business Brief

**Dataset:** Generated synthetic e-commerce order data  
**Period:** January 2024 – December 2025  
**Purpose:** Identify revenue trends, category performance, and high-value customers.

## Key findings

- **Total revenue:** ₹10,648,044.35 across **2,400 unique orders**.
- **Customers:** 384 unique customers; average order value was **₹4,436.69**.
- **Top category:** **Electronics**, generating ₹4,108,595.92 (38.6% of revenue).
- **Best revenue month:** **2025-12**, with revenue of ₹640,750.67.
- **Customer concentration:** The top 20% of customers generated **62.2%** of total revenue. The highest-spending customer was **CUST0419** (₹467,551.99).
- **Year-over-year change:** Revenue in 2025 was **21.0%** higher than in 2024.

## Recommended actions

1. **Prioritise the leading category.** Review stock availability and test targeted promotions for Electronics; monitor revenue and margin together before increasing spend.
2. **Plan around seasonal peaks.** Prepare inventory and campaign calendars ahead of the strongest revenue month (2025-12); compare multiple years before assuming the pattern will repeat.
3. **Retain valuable customers.** Test a loyalty benefit or personalised offers for high-value customers, while tracking repeat purchase rate and offer cost.
4. **Broaden the customer base.** Since the top 20% contribute 62.2% of revenue, run experiments to increase repeat purchases among mid-value customers and reduce reliance on a small group.
5. **Improve future analysis.** Add profit, discounts, returns, acquisition channel, and customer purchase dates to understand profitability and retention more fully.

## Data note and limitations

This project uses **generated/simulated data**, not public or real customer records. Findings are illustrative and cannot be used as claims about a real e-commerce company. The dataset has no churn label, so this is a sales trend and customer-value analysis—not a predictive churn model.
